# Glossary

Terms as FloweySales uses them. Each entry points to the page that explains it.

**Administrator (SYS_ADMIN).** The role that sets up the system: AI endpoint, plugins, people, mail and updates. See [Settings and roles](settings.md).

**Agent node.** A flow step that asks the model a question. By default it has no tools and answers from its input. See [Flow Studio](flows.md).

**Agent team.** A set of fictional investor characters that each look at the same data from their own approach. See [Agents, teams and evidence](agents.md).

**Approval.** A decision a person must make before a flow step changes something outside the run. Pending ones are in the Inbox. See [Flow Studio](flows.md#approvals-and-the-inbox).

**Backtest.** A replay of a strategy over past prices. It tests an idea on the past and does not predict. See [Strategies and backtests](strategies.md).

**Benchmark.** The index a backtest is compared with.

**Citation check.** A check that cited sources were retrieved and that figures and quoted passages appear in them. It does not prove the source is right. See [Provenance and trust](provenance.md).

**Evidence mode.** How strictly the Research Agent must support its claims: Standard, Evidence only, or Primary sources only. See [Agents, teams and evidence](agents.md).

**Factor.** One of the six parts of a leaderboard score: Value, Quality, Growth, Momentum, Entry timing and Low risk. See [Leaderboard](leaderboard.md).

**Flow.** A saved, repeatable chain of data steps, agents and approvals, with versions and a run history. See [Flow Studio](flows.md).

**Grade.** A coarser reading of the leaderboard score, from A to E. See [Leaderboard](leaderboard.md).

**Model profile.** A named model choice, such as "Fast and cheap", prepared by an administrator so that users pick by name. See [Settings and roles](settings.md).

**Paper portfolio.** A practice portfolio with simulated fills. Nothing reaches a broker. See [More areas](more-areas.md).

**Plugin (provider plugin).** An installable program that supplies market data. Without one the data screens are empty. See [Settings and roles](settings.md).

**Provenance.** Where a figure came from, when, and by what method. See [Provenance and trust](provenance.md).

**Score.** A number from 0 to 100 showing how a company compares within the chosen list. See [Leaderboard](leaderboard.md).

**Survivorship bias.** A backtest on today's list leaves out companies that dropped out earlier, which can flatter the result. See [Strategies and backtests](strategies.md).

**UTC.** The time zone for every date and time in the app and in exports.

**Walk-forward test.** Fitting on one period and testing on the next. It is not offered for leaderboard strategies, and the result says why. See [Strategies and backtests](strategies.md#walk-forward-testing).
